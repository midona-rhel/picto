# Picto 0.6.17-alpha

* Folder imports now have a progress window with file counts, speed, and an estimate of the time left. Minimize it to keep browsing, then bring it back from the progress bar.
* Pause and resume imports, or stop and choose whether to keep or remove what was added. Removing an import leaves existing items and their metadata alone. Only one import can run at a time, including Picto Packs.
* Imports survive a restart: paused imports stay paused, and running imports pick up again.
* Files shared by multiple collections no longer need separate items. Overlapping collections are combined so their complete sets stay together, with tags, notes, links, folder memberships, and the highest rating preserved.
* Permanent deletion can now be undone during the current session, up to the limit you choose in Settings. Larger deletions warn you when they cannot be undone. Closing Picto clears that undo history and lets deleted files be cleaned up.
* Choose whether rejecting an Inbox item sends it to Trash or deletes it permanently. The keyboard shortcut follows your setting too.
* Undo and redo messages now explain what changed in plain language.
* Tag searches put exact matches first, making it easier to find a series without wading through character tags.
* Sidebar arrows respond to a single click, and open or collapsed folders stay that way between sessions. Duplicate counts also update when duplicates change.
* Quick Look keeps each image's own proportions as you accept or reject Inbox items. The review buttons stay rounded and only light up when pressed.
* Subscription counts keep track of what was imported, even after items are trashed or deleted, without repeatedly scanning the full download history.
* On macOS, the Dock badge clears when you return to Picto, and the app icon has a larger, unified book design.

The first open after upgrading backs up your database and combines existing copies. This can take extra time in large libraries. Keep the backup: updated libraries may not open in older Picto versions.
