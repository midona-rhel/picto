# Picto 0.6.12-alpha

* Flash thumbnails now generate silently, and hidden players close when finished.
* Fixed Pixiv animation imports that could freeze during WebM playback on Windows.
* Subscription retries now recognize existing files even after you reorganize their groups.
* Subscription progress now shows how many files are already in your library.
* Picto now uses your library theme from startup, without briefly switching from an old theme.
* Removed obsolete settings that could interfere with your current preferences.
* Fixed a Windows error when refreshing a library’s folder icon.
* Improved playback error reporting to make troublesome files easier to diagnose.
* What’s new now appears on the first launch of each version on Windows, macOS, and Linux, including after manual installs and while offline.
