# Picto 0.6.18-alpha

* Accepting or rejecting an Inbox item in Quick Look or the viewer moves to the next item instantly. The image no longer flashes back to the previous one after an undo, and the review buttons stay on screen between items.
* A scroll that was still coasting when you reviewed an item no longer carries on into the next one.
* If a review can't be saved, Picto goes back to that item. While a delete confirmation is open, review shortcuts wait for your answer.
* The app icon's badge shows how many items are in your Inbox, and updates as items arrive or leave.
* Pawchive downloads work again instead of failing with "403 Forbidden".
* A subscription run no longer stops with "unsettled media" when more than one file in a post fails to download. Failed files are retried on the next run.
* FANBOX posts no longer import the post's cover thumbnail, only the post's own images and files.
* Konachan, Sankaku, Idol Complex and Baraag are no longer supported. Their subscriptions are paused with a note, and their saved sign-ins are removed. Items they already imported stay in your library.
