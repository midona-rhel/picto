# Collections

[← User Guide](README.md)

A collection keeps several media items together as one item in the grid, for example the pages of
a comic or the images of one post. The menus call a collection a group. A collection has a name,
notes, a cover and its own order of members. Its tags, its folders, its rating and its state
(Inbox, Active or Trash) belong to the collection as a whole.

## Create a collection

1. Select two or more items in the grid.
2. Right-click the selection and choose **Group...**.
3. Enter a name. You can also enter **Collection notes**. Then click **Create Group**.

The first selected item in the order of the grid becomes the cover.

Picto also creates a collection when you import several files and answer **Yes** to **Import as
Collection?** (see [Importing Files](importing-files.md#several-files-at-once)).
[Subscriptions](subscriptions.md) create a collection for a post that has several files.

### Add items to an existing collection

Select the collection together with the items to add, right-click the selection and choose **Merge
into One Group...**. The dialog **Choose the Group to Keep** lists the collections in the
selection. Choose one of them. That collection keeps its name, its cover, its order, its folders
and its state, and every other selected item is added at its end. Click **Merge Groups**.

## View a collection

Double-click a collection or press `Enter`. The reader shows the members one under the other in a
column that scrolls.

- Double-click a member to open it in the media view.
- `Left` and `Right` go to the previous and the next item of the grid.
- `Escape` closes the reader.
- **Open in New Window** (`Mod+O`) opens the collection in a window of its own.

A PDF file, a DjVu file or a comic archive that is a member shows all of its pages, one under the
other.

## Edit a collection

Right-click the collection and choose **Edit Group**, or click **Edit group** in the title bar
while the reader is open. The editor shows the members as a grid.

| Task | How |
|------|-----|
| Change the order of the members | Drag a tile to its new place. |
| Sort the members | Right-click and choose **Sort by**, then Name or Size. |
| Take members out of the collection | Select them and press `Delete` or `Backspace`, or right-click them and choose **Remove from Group**. They become separate items of the library. |
| Move members to the Trash | Right-click them and choose **Move to Trash**. They leave the collection and go to the Trash. |
| Open a member | Double-click it. `Space` opens Quick Look when exactly one member is selected. |

`Escape` clears the selection. When nothing is selected, `Escape` leaves the editor.

## Ungroup

Right-click the collection and choose **Ungroup...**, or right-click empty space inside the open
collection and choose **Ungroup...**. Then confirm. Every member becomes a separate item of the
library. No file and no metadata is deleted.

Every change to a collection can be undone with `Mod+Z`.
