# Picto 0.6.19-alpha

* Pawchive subscriptions no longer stop with "400 Bad Request" after a creator's first 50 posts. Pawchive now only accepts page offsets in steps of 50; subscriptions that stopped continue where they left off.
* Subscriptions on Pawchive keep their place when posts are added or deleted between runs. If Picto can't tell where it was, it checks earlier posts again rather than skip any.
* Select several collections (or a mix of collections and single items) and choose **Ungroup Collections…** to dissolve them all in one step, undone in one step. Single items in the selection stay as they are, and each member keeps its own collection's tags.
* With collections in the selection, **Group…** now reads **Merge into One Group…**, since grouping combines them into one collection.
